Why certification programs stall

Enablement is a funnel
that leaks at the same four points.

Nobody knows what they don't know

A single score of “68%” tells a candidate they failed. It doesn't tell them which objective to study tonight, so they re-read everything — or nothing.

Question banks go stale

Our platform renames products every quarter. Static PDFs of practice questions start teaching Delta Live Tables and Workflows the week after we rename them.

Program owners fly blind

The sponsor who signed off on 90 seats can't answer “who is actually ready?” until exam day — which is exactly when it's too late to intervene.

Practice ≠ passing

Memorizing answers clears a mock test and fails the real one. Official exams are scenario-based; the gap only closes with hands-on work.

Certifica is built around the official AI Prep Guide: Any Databricks Certification — including its anti-hallucination safeguards and its renamed-product table, injected into every prompt.

How the assistant works

Five AI steps, one closed loop.

Each step is a separate call to the Foundation Model API with its own tutoring system prompt — not one chatbot pretending to do everything.

STEP 1

Diagnose

A full mock exam, with questions allocated across the official exam-guide domains by their real weights.

◆ AI-generated
STEP 2

Practice

Scenario-based questions on chosen topics and difficulty — from the curated bank or generated fresh.

◆ AI-generated
STEP 3

Repair

For every wrong answer: what the misconception was, why the right answer is right, and why the distractor was tempting.

◆ AI-tutored
STEP 4

Deep dive

Teaches one weak objective end to end — core concept, how it shows up on the exam, runnable code example.

◆ AI-tutored
STEP 5

Hands-on

6–10 lab tasks the candidate must be able to perform, each with steps and an official docs link.

◆ AI-tutored

The loop closes: results from steps 1–2 update the mastery model, which selects what steps 3–5 teach next, which feeds the next focused re-test. The sixth AI flow maps weak topics to the customer's own training classes.

Why we can trust what it teaches

An AI tutor that invents
an exam objective is worse than none.

Docs allowlist, not free recall

Deep-dive prompts are grounded in a versioned snapshot of the official docs.databricks.com/llms.txt index. The model may cite only from that real page list — it cannot guess a plausible URL.

Renamed-product table injected

Nine known renames (DLT → Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines, Workflows → Lakeflow Jobs, Repos → Git folders…) are pushed into every system prompt. This blocks the single most common hallucination.

Explicit anti-invention rules

The prompt forbids inventing objectives, features or products outside certification scope, and forbids third-party blogs and forums as sources.

Human-reviewed floor

613 curated questions and 200 flashcards ship as the baseline, so the product is useful and correct even before a single AI call.

Why this scales across the field

One deployment.
Every account.

Certifica was built multi-tenant from day one. Onboarding a new customer is configuration, not a fork — so the effort we spend improving it compounds across every account instead of dying in one repo.

  • Runtime branding. Color, logo and name are applied per tenant at request time — a single build serves every customer, on their own brand.
  • Shared question bank, isolated results. Certifications and questions are global; users, sessions and answers carry a tenant ID. Improving content lifts every customer at once.
  • Three access levels. Platform operator (manage customers) · tenant admin (manage their people, export PDF/CSV) · trainee (study). Self-service signup optional per tenant.
  • It's a Databricks Apps reference implementation. Every customer demo doubles as a live proof point for Apps, Lakebase and the Foundation Model API.
